Description

[This aura is not affected by any kind of luck.] : No potions / gauntlets help with rolling this aura A being frozen long ago. However, its frozen heart continued to beat, and a hunter with a frozen heart was born. It is a cruel sentinel that freezes everything in sight, it prioritizes eliminating beings with warm hearts. Unless there was something to melt it's heart, it would continue on its way as a hunter. Passive - [Frostbite]‎ Every attack Cryogenic does inflicts [Frostbite] stack based on the damage dealt. When [Frostbite] stack reaches 100, the victim frozes for 5 seconds, making them unable to move. If the [Frostbite] stack remains unchanged for more then 10 seconds, the stack is removed.
