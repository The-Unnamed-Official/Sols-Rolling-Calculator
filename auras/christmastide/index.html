Description

"Wow, it feels like we're already standing at the edge of the end of all four seasons. It's something I feel every year—while a year feels long as you're living through it, it somehow feels unbelievably short when you look back on it.

What have you accomplished this year? Surely, there must have been some incredible moments, right?
Even if they weren't something huge, that's okay. Small memories can still hold great meaning.

Nothing happened at all? Congratulations! Just making it through without anything bad happening is already a success!
Was it full of bad things instead? Oh no... but don't worry. Bad things can happen to anyone. When people go through hardships, the pain often makes them forget all the good things they experienced as well. So please, hold on to even the smallest bits of happiness. I'll be rooting for those little joys to grow like a snowball and come back to you as something truly great.

With that, I hope you have a wonderful end to the year. Merry Christmas!" -
- Someone who loves Christmas -
