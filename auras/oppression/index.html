Description

"Once in the past, there existed a being with immense and unstable power, strong enough to drive the world to the brink of collapse.

This being left cracks across the world and gradually expanded its influence.

The world's authorities united to stop it, using every means available, but ultimately, they failed.

In the end, 「God」 confronted the being, and though the being defied even 「God」, when 「God」 drew His sword and struck, all the cracks left by the being were wiped clean.

It seemed like everything had finally ended. The world regained its peace, and authorities sighed in relief.

...But is this truly the end?

The remnants of that power did not disappear. Perhaps this is just the beginning.

This story... is not over yet."
