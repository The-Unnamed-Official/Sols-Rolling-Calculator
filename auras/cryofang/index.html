Description

Snow fell without sound, burying the world beneath layers of pale bule silence as the cold grew deeper with every passing second. The air cracked with frozen energy, and within a radiant circle of ice, CryoFang slowly took shape, its presence warping frost and light alike. Each deliberate movement sent sharp ripples across the ground, as if the land itself acknowledged its return. Its glowing blue eyes held no rage, only an ancient duty to guard a realm forgotten by time and memory. When the blizzard reached its peak and the sky vanished into white, CryoFang dissolved into the storm, becoming nothing more than a whisper of eternal winter.
