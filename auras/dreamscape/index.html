Description

*DAY 109* I keep having the same dream, over and over again. In it, I'm always walking along an endless road. I can't tell whatever it's day or night, but the sky glows with an artificial light, and thick fog blankets everything. Street lamps flicker sporadically along the path, accompanied by scattered road signs. But I've never seen a car, a person, or even a single thing moving. Each time I dream, the duration seems to stretch longer. At first, it felt like less than an hour. But now... It feels like decades have passed. There seems to be nothing allowed to me but walking. Or maybe I've long since forgotten how to even try doing anything else. When I walk in this dream, I count the seconds. 2,207,568,830... I think it's been over seventy years. Please, someone get me out of here. Where is this? Free me. What is happening to me? Let me out. Who am I? Help me please... Maybe death would be better than this...
