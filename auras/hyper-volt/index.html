Description

A phenomenon witnessed many times, yet still doubted by science.
Believed to be the clusters of low-temperature plasma forming sudden flashes mid-air.
Those clustered energies create Hyper Volt—so powerful it can be mistaken for a natural disaster.
It releases chaotic electric currents and circles unpredictably around its surroundings.
