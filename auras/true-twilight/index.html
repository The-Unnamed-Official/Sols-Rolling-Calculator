Description

He calls her, "Twilight."

She was a quite, fragile girl, with a world of wonder locked behind her eyes. It was never her fault that tragedy found her so soon, stripping away her family and her home in a war she was too small to understand.
Escaping into the deep woods that night, she left behind a burning world.

At the heart of the trees, the Jewel of the Moon found her in the dark. It offered her peace. She accepted with all of her heart, to become the youngest of its guardians.

For a long time, she did not feel alone. She found purpose in that duty. She tended to the pale flora, learning the gentle rhythm of the night, and guiding lost wanderers back to safety. Even as her kin faded into stone one by one, leaving her as the solitary keeper of a silent forest, she did not despair. She did not wait for a savior. She simply tended her garden, content to be the light in the dark for anyone who crossed her threshold.

Little did the forest know, and little did she know, the most fragile among them would end up carrying the weight of eternity, over and over.
