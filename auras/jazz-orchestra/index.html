Description

As the age of prosperity showed its glory around the world, together with it came music. Melodies flowed through the streets of every town, small and big, slow and fast, cold and hot, bringing hope across the countless minds of the passerbys. For the first couple of years however, music was never considered an actual profession, rather a difficult to practice hobby, until she came. She knew her musical talent and that of everyone else wasn't just a hobby anymore, it had to be much more. So, she gathered the greatest composers and musicians she knew from one street to another and created what was known as the Orchestra.
Their strength together managed to let out such melodies, calm and excited, contemplative and brisk, melancholic and euphoric, that they were called upon the courts of numerous rulers just for their music, inspiring others in the way.
Only one however, they could not satisfy, leaving a frown of failure on them all.
