The light shimmered in his eye.
Born to swim, and yet all he could do is watch as the sea passed him by. There he sat, alone but not afraid; for time has rusted his fear.

And there, his arms threw themselves out.
They lunged towards that shadow, a figure drifting through the waters.
He held, oh he held. A tight grip, a firm clutch. He watched as the light shone on the figure, a lifeless body yet still so warm.
He held that body in his arms and there he closed his eyes, his soul reaching for the remnants of what was once a life once broken.
