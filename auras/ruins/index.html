Description

In the ruins of an ancient civilization, destroyed by the destructive rituals and dark sorcery, only a place known as the Sanctuary remains, though it has crumbled in form.
For countless years after the civilization's downfall, the Sanctuary became a resting place for spirits. There, the spirits lingered, harmonizing the thoughts of the ancient people with their own magical essence and will, bathing the ruins in a turquoise glow
It is said that those who encounter or step into this place will regain inner peace and tranquility
