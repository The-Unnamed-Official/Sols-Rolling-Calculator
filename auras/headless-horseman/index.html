Description

On All Hallows' Eve, the Headless Horseman rises, carrying an pumpkin he stole to replace the head he once had.
Once a soldier betrayed in battle, he now roams the night as a vengeful spirit, hunting any living soul who disturbs the dead.
As he passes the graveyard, he senses a lingering green presence of a dark spirit that was there not long ago. An unseen veil seems to guide his path, drawing him ever onward.
Along his journey, he may encounter forces as dark as himself. when dawn breaks, he vanishes, only to rise again, next Halloween.
