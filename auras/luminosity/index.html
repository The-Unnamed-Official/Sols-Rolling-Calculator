Description

"Audite me, arma mea. Quaerite semina chaos in hoc mundo relicta, et amne chaos purgate."

The era of primordial chaos came to an end by the hands of Sol.

Sol knew—no, he could feel—that fragments of the chaos he had annihilated still lingered in the world. He understood that his mission would not be complete until every last trace of it was purged. Yet the remnants of chaos were too small, too well hidden, for even him to find.

Thus, Sol bestowed consciousness upon his weapons, leaving them behind as his legacy—so that even after his disappearance, they would continue the eternal hunt for chaos.

However, an unforeseen variable arose. Chaos began to grow its power much faster than Sol had anticipated. The other weapons, scattered across the world in pursuit of the seeds of chaos, were destroyed by its resurgence—until only one remained.

The sword that carried the brightest light.

The blade Luminosity, symbol of Sol's purest power, bears a name that is not only its own but also that of a transcendent being dwelling within it. Now, it stands as Sol's final legacy—and the only key to the tomb where he rests.

For countless ages, the sword has continued its search, seeking both the shattered remnants of its kin and the dormant seeds of chaos.
