Description

When still too young to bear the weight of sins, yet far too inexperienced to have earned virtues, the soul of the purest among beings is called to ascend beyond the mortal realm long before they should've, departing from a world they barely began to know.
The ones below are forced to hold their cries, surrendering their sorrow to the quiet, to let them fly higher, believing that each tear would slow the beings down. So, the souls continue to ascend beyond the mortal's expectations, reaching levels the mythology hid in its depths.
