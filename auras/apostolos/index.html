Description

Apostolos. order and chaos are diametrically opposed concepts that can never coexist. However, there exists an exceptional being that embodies both order and chaos. In mythological depictions, Apostolos represents the intersection of order and chaos. In his right hand rests the curse of chaos, and in his left hand, the blessing of order. This powerful being roams the fates, delivering their judgement.
