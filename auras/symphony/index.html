Description

"What.. have I done..."

In his final moments, a sorrow-filled string melody reaches the sinner's ears.

As he reflects upon his actions, he finally realizes his own ignorance. He laments his foolishness—but it is already far too late.

The past cannot be undone.
The sinner senses the end drawing near, and he accepts it.

At that moment, the music stops.
And with tears in her eyes, the angel draws her blade toward the sinner.
