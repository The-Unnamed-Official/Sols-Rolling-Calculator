Description

The raven is known as a harbinger of misfortune or disaster. Its instinct to sense calamity before others and flee, its tendency to travel in flocks, its dark an ominous presence, its habit of feeding on corpses, and its exceptional intelligence all contribute to this image. Some even believe that ravens are evildoers cursed by witches and turned into black birds.
