Description

[Cycle 8,128]
He did not reach for the jewel.
He did not ask the sky for another thousand years.
He understood, at last, the cruelty of a stasis that refused to let go.
And so, to all that mattered to him, he gave his final grace.

He let the Crimson Canopy finally shatter. He let all of their memories finally go, one last time.

With no memory for the world to hold onto, the forest began to heal itself of their history.
The statues crumbled into glowing mist. The deep carvings knitted into untouched bark. The colors lifted from the cave walls, everything carried away by a wind that no longer needed to listen.

Her form dissolved into a cascade of bioluminescent light, ascending from the earth, leaving only a single blue flower to sway in the sudden quiet.

The world was forgetting them. Willingly. Peacefully.

In the center of the radiant, erased clearing, the fabric of the dark tore open.
A rift of pure, silver light spilled across the grass: a pathway to the true moon.

He stood before the threshold. He no longer carried the weight of eight million years in his chest, a treasure the earth could no longer hold.

He did not look back at the Radiant Forest.
He gazed only into the silver expanse. There, bathed in the pale, quiet light, she was waiting for him.

As he stepped into the veil, he whispered into the jewel: "There will never be another Tomorrow. "
