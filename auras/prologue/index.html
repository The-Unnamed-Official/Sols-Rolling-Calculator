Description

The long-forgotten remnants of what was once the Chronicle of the World, [PROLOGUE], now lie scattered amidst the suffocating darkness of The Limbo.

Conceived as a gift, it was painstakingly brought into existence by the [God of Space], with its pages crafted piece by piece in the earnest hope of aiding the [God of Knowledge] in its ceaseless pursuit of knowledge.
Misguided by its silence, the [God of Space] mistook the [God of Knowledge]'s solitary nature for unwavering devotion to its work, never realizing that its efforts would one day be met not with gratitude, but with rejection.
As [PROLOGUE] grew, the knowledge inscribed within its never-ending pages kept growing at rates for higher than what the [God of Space] had ever intended.

To the [God of Knowledge], its glowing brilliance was nothing short of an affront.
What right did this mere collection of borrowed hands have to flourish so magnificently, when its very existence had been deemed unnecessary by them?
Unable to tolerate the shadow it cost upon its own pursuits, the [God of Knowledge] condemned the Chronicle to The Limbo, casting it beyond the boundaries of the known world, where time nor memory could reach.

There, its pages were scattered, its knowledge abandoned to the endless silence between realities.
Its name faded from the tongues of the living, and its existence become little more than a forgotten footnote in the annals of creation.
